I am an independent researcher in applied mathematics and computational linguistics. My current work focuses on the Engineering of Alphabets (EOA) Program — a research program on deterministic symbolic representations derived from letter-name strings.

Research

The EOA Program

The EOA Program investigates a class of deterministic symbolic representations whose algebraic properties depend on the encoding in a globally-coupled way. The work spans:

  • Symbolic dynamics and convergence ratios.
  • Low-rank structural primitives and memory efficiency.
  • Cross-encoding variation and phonetics.
